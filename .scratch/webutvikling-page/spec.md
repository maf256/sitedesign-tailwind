**Goal:** Add a `/webutvikling` route (NO on sitedesign.no, EN on en.sitedesign.no) with SEO-optimised content for the high-value keyword "webutvikling".

**Relation to `/webutvikler`:** `/webutvikler` already exists (role, hire, career intent). `/webutvikling` is a separate page for the service/process intent: what web development is, how a project runs, technologies, project cost, choosing an agency or consultant. ## Division of labour (applies to every ticket)

- **Text content** (copy, headings, meta text, FAQ answers, NO and EN) is generated by the **Gemini "Argon" sub agent**. Never hand-write it or have a coding model write it.
- **Implementation (actual code)** goes to **Sonnet or Opus**, chosen by complexity. Each ticket has a `Model:` line. Routing, wiring and rendering: Sonnet. Schema/JSON-LD correctness and anything with subtle parity or validation rules: Opus.
- Gemini output is handed over as text (JSON-ready strings). The coding model puts it into the lang files and does not rewrite it, except to fix a factual or rendering problem, and then reports the change.
