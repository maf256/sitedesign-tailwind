# Spec: /webutvikling page

**Goal:** Add a `/webutvikling` route (NO on sitedesign.no, EN on en.sitedesign.no) with SEO-optimised content for the high-value keyword "webutvikling".

**Relation to `/webutvikler`:** `/webutvikler` already exists (role, hire, career intent). `/webutvikling` is a separate page for the service/process intent: what web development is, how a project runs, technologies, project cost, choosing an agency or consultant. The two pages cross-link and must not duplicate copy (keyword cannibalization risk).

## Division of labour (applies to every ticket)

- **Text content** (copy, headings, meta text, FAQ answers, NO and EN) is generated by the **Gemini "Argon" sub agent**. Never hand-write it or have a coding model write it.
- **Implementation (actual code)** goes to **Sonnet or Opus**, chosen by complexity. Each ticket has a `Model:` line. Routing, wiring and rendering: Sonnet. Schema/JSON-LD correctness and anything with subtle parity or validation rules: Opus.
- Gemini output is handed over as text (JSON-ready strings). The coding model puts it into the lang files and does not rewrite it, except to fix a factual or rendering problem, and then reports the change.

### 2. Intent split table: `/webutvikling` vs. `/webutvikler`

To prevent keyword cannibalization and duplicated copy, `/webutvikling` focuses strictly on **the discipline, delivery process, architectural choices, project economics, and buying model**, whereas `/webutvikler` focuses on **the individual professional role, career path, personal skills, and hiring a person**.

| Topic / Query Cluster | Belongs to `/webutvikling` (Service, Process & Project Delivery) | Stays on `/webutvikler` (Role, Person, Skills & Career) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core definition** | `hva er webutvikling`, `utvikling av nettsider og webapplikasjoner`: The engineering discipline and lifecycle of turning business goals into working web software (static sites, CMS-driven sites, custom web apps). | `hva er en webutvikler`, `hva gjør en webutvikler`: The person/profession, daily tasks, and human problem-solving skills. |
| **Frontend & Backend** | **System & architecture angle:** Client rendering vs. server logic, APIs, databases, edge hosting, and how the layers connect in a digital product. | **Role specialization angle:** What a frontend developer vs. backend developer vs. fullstack developer works on day-to-day. |
| **Workflow & Process** | `webutvikling prosess`: The 5-stage project lifecycle (1. Behov & krav / Discovery, 2. UX/UI-design i Figma, 3. Utvikling & integrasjoner, 4. Testing, SEO & universell utforming / WCAG, 5. Lansering & drift). | *Not covered on `/webutvikler`* (which instead covers the 5 steps to *become* a web developer: `hvordan bli webutvikler`). |
| **Technology stack** | **Project-fit & selection angle:** Choosing the right stack for the business case — when to pick WordPress vs. headless CMS (Sanity) vs. custom React/Next.js + Node.js/TypeScript, and why Core Web Vitals/SEO matter. | **Practitioner toolbelt angle:** The tools Majid personally masters as an Oslo developer (`Teknologier en moderne webutvikler bruker`). |
| **Pricing & Cost** | `hva koster webutvikling`, `hva koster en nettside`: Project-scoped cost drivers (scope, design complexity, CMS vs. custom logic, integrations, WCAG/SEO, ongoing hosting & maintenance) + project price tiers (simple corporate site ~15k–50k NOK, custom web app/integrations 50k+ NOK, consulting rate ~800–1 500 NOK/hr ex. VAT). | `hva koster det å hyre en webutvikler`: Rate and hiring cost of bringing a developer onto a task or team. |
| **Buying / Sourcing** | `velge webbyrå eller konsulent`: Comparing delivery models for a business buyer (large agency vs. senior consultant via OsloWeb AS vs. template/no-code builders), governance, SLA, and code/domain ownership. | `frilans, konsulent eller fast ansatt`, `velge riktig webutvikler`: Employment forms and how to evaluate/interview an individual developer. |
| **Career & Education** | *Excluded* — link readers with career/education intent to `/webutvikler`. | `hvordan bli webutvikler`, learning HTML/CSS/JS, building a GitHub portfolio, applying for junior roles. |
